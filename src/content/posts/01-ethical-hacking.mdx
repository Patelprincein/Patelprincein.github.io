Spending evenings inside **TryHackMe** rooms completely changed how I look at product engineering.

1. I write a threat story first.
2. I set up reproducible environments with disposable Kali boxes.
3. I document everything inside a git-backed journal.

```bash
nmap -sC -sV 10.10.154.236 -oN recon/nmap.txt
```

The tooling I automate along the way often becomes a public repo. My latest obsession is turning every lab into a *repeatable* mini-playbook I can share with classmates.

> Pro tip: narrate what you expect **before** running a command. When the results differ, new ideas show up instantly.
